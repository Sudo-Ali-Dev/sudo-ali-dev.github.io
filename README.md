# Muhammad Ali's Digital Space

Welcome to my personal corner of the internet.

## What is this?
This is my portfolio, blog, and digital archive. It houses my projects (like Rankistan), my writings on engineering and life, and a "digital Ali" AI bot you can chat with.

## The Intuition
"Chase Entropy."
I wanted a space that feels like home—fast, minimal, and terminal-inspired. It's designed to document the journey, map the unseen, and catalog the things I build without any unnecessary friction or noise.
