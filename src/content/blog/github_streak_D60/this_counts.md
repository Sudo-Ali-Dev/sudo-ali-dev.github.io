60 days.

Didn’t plan it.
Just didn’t stop.

Some days I built things that actually mattered.
Some days I just showed up so the square wouldn’t go dark.

Not every day was meaningful.
Still counts.

No one was watching.
No one needed to.

There’s something strange about seeing that line stay unbroken.
Quiet proof that you were there. Every day.

Nothing dramatic happened.
Just consistency stacking up in the background.

Anyway.

60 days done.

Not stopping.
